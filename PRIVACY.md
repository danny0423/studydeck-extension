# Privacy Policy — StudyDeck 單字收集

Last updated: 2026-11-26

This extension saves words you select on web pages to **your own StudyDeck account**, on the StudyDeck server address you enter in the extension's settings.

## What the extension handles

| Data | When | Where it goes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The word you select, and the sentence around it on the page | Only when you click "加到 StudyDeck 待學習" in the right-click menu | Sent to the StudyDeck server you set, to look up its meaning and save it to your to-learn list |
| Your StudyDeck username/email and password | Only when you log in on the settings page | Sent to the StudyDeck server you set. The password is not stored by the extension |
| StudyDeck server address and login token | After you log in | Stored only in your browser (`chrome.storage.local`) |

## What it does not do

- It does not read pages you don't select text on, and does not track browsing history.
- It does not send data anywhere except the StudyDeck server address you enter.
- It has no analytics, ads, or third-party tracking.
- It does not sell or transfer your data to third parties.

The StudyDeck server you connect to may use an AI service to explain words. That is handled by that server, under its own policy.

## Removing your data

Log out on the settings page, or remove the extension, to delete the stored address and token from your browser. Words already saved can be deleted in StudyDeck's to-learn list.

## Contact

Open an issue in this extension's GitHub repository.

## 隱私權政策（中文）

這個擴充功能會把你在網頁上選的字，存到**你自己的 StudyDeck 帳號**，也就是你在設定頁填的那個 StudyDeck 網址。

- **選的字和那一句**：只有在你按右鍵選單「加到 StudyDeck 待學習」時，才會送到你設定的 StudyDeck，用來查意思、存進待學習。
- **帳號和密碼**：只有在設定頁登入時才會送到你設定的 StudyDeck。擴充功能不會存密碼。
- **網址和登入憑證**：只存在你的瀏覽器裡。
- 不讀你沒選字的網頁、不記錄瀏覽紀錄、不送到其他地方、沒有分析或廣告、不賣給第三方。
- 你連的 StudyDeck 可能會用 AI 解釋單字，那是 StudyDeck 那邊的處理。
- 在設定頁登出、或移除擴充功能，就會刪掉存在瀏覽器裡的資料。已經存的字可以在 StudyDeck 的待學習刪掉。
